How it works

One pack in. A cleared hire out.

01

Collect the onboarding pack

Offer letter, ID, work authorization, banking, tax forms — any format, or emailed straight in.

02

Validate completeness & validity

The AI confirms signatures and dates, checks work authorization isn't expired, and matches the name across documents.

03

Cleared, or auto follow-up

A clear verdict per hire. Anything outstanding emails the new hire; the file waits and re-checks itself.
