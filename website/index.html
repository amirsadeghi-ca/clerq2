FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you only build with your own products?⌄

No. Clerq2 and Vision are great starting points when they fit, but a large part of our work is bespoke — custom apps, integrations and automation built around your process. We recommend the cheapest path to the outcome, even when that isn't one of our products.

Can you work with public sector procurement?⌄

Yes. We're a Canadian studio used to working within procurement, security and compliance constraints, and we deliver in both English and French. We can engage as a prime or as a specialist subcontractor on AI components.

How do you keep AI decisions defensible?⌄

Every result carries its evidence and its source, a human reviews and can override with a reason, and the whole thing is auditable and exportable. The AI's original answer is preserved alongside the human decision — so you can always explain a verdict later.

Where does our data live, and which models do you use?⌄

It depends on your requirements. We're model-agnostic and can deploy to your cloud, a managed environment, or on-prem where needed. We'll design the data-residency and model choices around your security and compliance posture, not the other way around.

What does a first engagement look like?⌄

A short, fixed-scope discovery: we look at one real process, map the use case and the risks, and come back with a prototype plan and an honest read on whether AI is the right tool. From there we build in clear phases.

Let's put AI to work on one real process

Bring us one workflow — the documents or images, the rules, the exceptions. We'll show you what production-grade AI looks like for it, end to end. We reply within one business day.
